Welcome to

Women of Purpose

At Women of Purpose, we believe that every person is created by God with purpose, potential, and value. We believe that each of us is born with unique gifts and talents, and throughout life we are given assignments, opportunities, and experiences that help prepare us for the purpose God has designed for us.

However, life's challenges, disappointments, inequalities, and circumstances can often distract, discourage, or prevent people from recognising their worth and pursuing their God-given calling. This is especially true for those living in marginalised and underserved communities, where opportunities and support may be limited.

Established in the United Kingdom in 2007 and expanded to Ghana in 2010, Women of Purpose exists to equip, support, mentor, and inspire women and young people to aspire, achieve, and fulfil their purpose. Through faith-based mentoring, leadership development, wellbeing initiatives, skills-building programmes, and community projects, we help individuals build confidence, overcome barriers, and discover their potential.

Our heart is for people and communities. We are passionate about reaching those who are often overlooked and creating opportunities that empower individuals to thrive. Through our work in communities including Cape Coast, Ghana, we support women and young people to develop their gifts, strengthen their resilience, and become positive contributors within their families and communities.

We believe that empowered people transform communities. By investing in individuals, nurturing leadership, and encouraging purpose-driven living, we help create lasting change that extends beyond the individual to future generations.
