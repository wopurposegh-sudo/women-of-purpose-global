About Women of Purpose Global

Women of Purpose Global is a community-led organisation established in the United Kingdom in 2014 and expanded to Ghana later that same year. We exist to empower women and young people to thrive through leadership development, wellbeing support, mentoring, skills-building, and community engagement programmes.

Our work is rooted in the belief that every individual has potential, purpose, and a valuable voice within their community. We create safe, inclusive, and supportive spaces where women and young people can build confidence, develop life skills, strengthen their wellbeing, and access opportunities that enable them to reach their full potential.

Over the years, we have delivered a range of community-based initiatives that support personal development, employability, education, social inclusion, and positive mental wellbeing. Our programmes are designed with and for the communities we serve, ensuring that participants play an active role in shaping activities, services, and future opportunities.

A key focus of our work is youth engagement. Through youth-led projects, mentoring programmes, leadership workshops, creative activities, and community events, we empower young people to become active contributors and future leaders within their communities. We believe that young people are not simply beneficiaries of services but partners in creating meaningful and lasting change.

Our approach combines practical support, peer learning, community participation, and positive role modelling. We work collaboratively with families, schools, community organisations, faith groups, and local stakeholders to strengthen support networks and improve outcomes for women and young people.

Today, Women of Purpose Global continues to champion empowerment, inclusion, and community transformation across the UK and Ghana, helping individuals build confidence, develop skills, and create brighter futures for themselves and their communities.

Our Mission

To empower women and young people through mentoring, leadership development, wellbeing support, skills-building, and community engagement opportunities that promote confidence, resilience, inclusion, and positive life outcomes.

Our Vision

A world where women and young people are empowered, heard, and equipped to lead positive change within their communities and beyond.

Our Values

  • Empowerment – Supporting individuals to recognise and achieve their potential.
  • Inclusion – Creating welcoming spaces where every voice matters.
  • Leadership – Developing confident leaders who inspire positive change.
  • Wellbeing – Promoting healthy, resilient, and thriving communities.
  • Community – Building meaningful connections and strengthening local networks.
  • Participation – Ensuring women and young people help shape the decisions and programmes that affect them.
