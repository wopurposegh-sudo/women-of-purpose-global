About Women of Purpose Global

Women of Purpose Global is a community-led organisation established in the United Kingdom in 2007 and expanded to Ghana in 2010. Since our inception, we have been committed to empowering women and young people through leadership development, mentoring, wellbeing support, skills-building opportunities, and community engagement programmes.

Founded on the belief that every individual has value, purpose, and the ability to create positive change, we work alongside communities to provide safe, inclusive spaces where women and young people can build confidence, develop practical skills, strengthen resilience, and realise their full potential.

Over the years, Women of Purpose Global has evolved from a grassroots support network into an organisation delivering community-led initiatives that promote personal development, education, employability, social inclusion, health and wellbeing, and leadership growth. Our programmes are shaped by the voices and experiences of the people we serve, ensuring that activities remain relevant, accessible, and impactful.

Youth participation sits at the heart of our work. Through mentoring programmes, leadership development activities, creative projects, workshops, volunteering opportunities, and youth-led community initiatives, we empower young people to become active contributors and future leaders. We believe that young people should not only participate in programmes but also influence, design, and shape them.

Our approach combines mentoring, peer support, community participation, and collaborative partnerships. We work closely with families, schools, community groups, faith organisations, and local stakeholders to strengthen support systems and create opportunities that improve outcomes for women and young people.

Today, Women of Purpose Global continues to champion empowerment, inclusion, wellbeing, and community transformation across the UK and Ghana. Through our programmes and partnerships, we help women and young people build confidence, develop skills, amplify their voices, and become positive agents of change within their communities.

Our Mission

To empower women and young people through mentoring, leadership development, wellbeing support, skills-building opportunities, and community engagement programmes that promote confidence, resilience, inclusion, participation, and positive life outcomes.

Our Vision

A world where women and young people are empowered, valued, and equipped to lead positive change, strengthen their communities, and achieve their full potential.

Our Values

  • Empowerment – Supporting individuals to recognise and achieve their potential.
  • Inclusion – Creating welcoming spaces where every voice matters.
  • Leadership – Developing confident leaders who inspire positive change.
  • Wellbeing – Promoting healthy, resilient, and thriving communities.
  • Community – Building meaningful connections and strengthening local networks.
  • Participation – Ensuring women and young people help shape the decisions and programmes that affect them.
