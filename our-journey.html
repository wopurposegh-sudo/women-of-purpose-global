Our Journey

Empowering women and young people across the UK and Ghana through faith, mentoring, leadership, wellbeing, skills development and community-led programmes since 2007.

About Women of Purpose

Women of Purpose is a Christian, community-led organisation established in the United Kingdom in 2007 and expanded to Ghana in 2010. We exist to equip, support, mentor and inspire women and young people to discover their God-given purpose, develop their potential and become positive contributors within their communities.

Our work is rooted in the belief that every person is created by God with value, gifts and purpose. Inspired by Ephesians 2:10, we believe purpose is a process. The assignments, opportunities, challenges and seasons we encounter in life help build our skills, sharpen our gifts and bring greater clarity to the purpose God has placed within us.

Life can bring distractions, discouragement, inequalities and circumstances that cause people to lose confidence, direction or hope. This is especially true for women and young people in marginalised and underserved communities where encouragement, opportunity and support may be limited.

Through mentoring, leadership development, wellbeing initiatives, skills-building programmes, faith-based support, creative activities and community projects, Women of Purpose helps individuals grow in confidence, overcome barriers and walk boldly in the assignments prepared for them.

Youth-led work is central to our passion. We believe young people are not only the leaders of tomorrow, but leaders today. Our desire is to create opportunities where young people can use their voices, shape programmes, develop leadership skills and influence positive change in their communities.

We are especially committed to reaching marginalised communities, including our current community work in Cape Coast, Ghana. By walking alongside communities, we support women and young people to develop resilience, strengthen their faith, discover their gifts and access meaningful opportunities.

Today, Women of Purpose continues to champion purpose, faith, empowerment, inclusion and community transformation. We believe that when people are mentored, encouraged and connected to opportunities, they become catalysts for lasting change within their families, communities and future generations.

Our Mission

To equip, support, mentor and inspire women and young people to discover their God-given purpose, build confidence, develop skills and access opportunities that enable them to thrive and create positive change.

Our Vision

A world where women and young people understand their value, walk confidently in their purpose and use their gifts to strengthen families, communities and future generations.

Our Values

  • Faith – Grounding our work in Christian values and the belief that every person is created with purpose.
  • Purpose – Helping individuals understand that purpose is a process shaped by life’s assignments and opportunities.
  • Mentorship – Supporting people through guidance, encouragement, lived experience and positive role modelling.
  • Youth Leadership – Creating youth-led opportunities where young people can influence, lead and grow.
  • Community – Reaching marginalised communities and building strong support networks.
  • Empowerment – Equipping women and young people with the confidence, skills and opportunities to thrive.
