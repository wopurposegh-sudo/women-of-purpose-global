About Our Founder

Pastor Amelia Quansah

Teacher • Mentor • Community Leader • Founder

Pastor Amelia Quansah is the Founder of Women of Purpose, a Christian community organisation established in the United Kingdom in 2007 and expanded to Ghana in 2010. Her passion is to see women and young people discover their God-given purpose, develop their potential, and become positive agents of change within their communities.

Inspired by Ephesians 2:10, Amelia believes that purpose is a process. She believes every person is created by God with purpose, but purpose unfolds over time through the assignments, opportunities, challenges, and experiences we encounter throughout life. These seasons build our skills, sharpen our gifts, strengthen our character, and bring greater clarity to the purpose God has placed within us.

For more than twenty years, Amelia has worked across education, children’s services, safeguarding, family support, and community development, supporting children, young people, women, and families from diverse backgrounds. Her own life journey has shaped a deep passion for encouraging and supporting those who may feel overlooked, discouraged, or uncertain about their future.

Youth development is at the heart of Amelia’s vision. She is passionate about creating opportunities for young people to lead, contribute, and use their voices to bring positive change within their communities. She believes that young people are not simply the leaders of tomorrow—they are leaders today.

Amelia is especially committed to serving marginalised communities where opportunity may be limited but potential is abundant. Through Women of Purpose, she works alongside local communities, schools, churches, families, and partner organisations to strengthen support networks and create pathways that enable women and young people to thrive. Current community projects include youth engagement and community development initiatives in Cape Coast, Ghana.

Alongside her community work, Amelia serves as an educator, mentor, preacher, and advocate, combining professional knowledge with lived experience to inspire hope, resilience, confidence, and faith. Her greatest desire is to connect people to their purpose through meaningful mentoring relationships and life-changing opportunities.

“Purpose is a process. The assignments we encounter in life build our skills, sharpen our gifts, and bring clarity to the purpose God has placed within us.”

Minister • Teacher • Mentor • Community Leader
